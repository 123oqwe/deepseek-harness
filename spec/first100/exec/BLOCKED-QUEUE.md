- **Resolution, effective immediately:**
  1. The already-corrected P0-03.C cell (re-greened with the real SHA `d7b1931eaeec211ff1eb0a9921308732f2592a0b` after catching the fabricated one) stands as-is -- no rollback needed, since the underlying CI observation was genuine and only the recorded label was wrong.
  2. **Tracked fix, deferred to the same safe breakpoint as BLOCKED-014's structural CI-fold** (not mid-slice): `generate-ledger.mjs`'s `--candidate-sha` handling gets two additional checks beyond the format regex -- (a) `git cat-file -e <sha>^{commit}` (the object exists and is a commit, not merely a well-formed hex string), and (b) reachability: the SHA must be a real ancestor of `first100-exec` (or an in-flight `attempt/*` branch), not an arbitrary disconnected object.
